# Island and peak knowledge: fact-check checklist

The kiosk shows what each island (and the main peaks) is known for: on the ferry screen's card while the ship passes,
and on the map labels. The entries are drafts, researched with web searches on 2026-10-17. **Every entry must be
checked by someone on the team before the kiosk goes live.**

- The text is in `en/landmarks.json` and `fr/landmarks.json`, under each place's `knowledge` key (same ids, and the
  same order and number of specialties in both languages).
- When an entry is checked, set `"checked": true` in **both** files. Development builds show a "DRAFT" chip on
  every entry that is still `false`.
- `indigenousName` is empty everywhere on purpose: fill it only from verified sources (the notes below flag two places
  whose official names now include a SENĆOŦEN name).
- `months` (1–12) marks events that happen in those months; in those months they come first, with a "This month"
  badge. Check event seasons each year.
- One change to existing text was made already: Sidney Island's fact no longer mentions the fallow deer herd (see
  "Left out on purpose" at the end).

## Left out on purpose (could not verify, outdated, or risky)

- **Sidney Island fallow deer**: the existing mock fact (`mocks/*/landmarks.json`) says Sidney Island has "a herd of fallow deer". Parks Canada began an eradication program (aerial and ground cull, Dec 2023; phase 2 halted Nov 2024). Numbers today are unknown and the topic is contested. **Suggest rewording that mock fact.**
- **Malaspina Galleries**: often wrongly linked to Galiano; they are on Gabriola Island.
- **Mount Maxwell "highest point on Salt Spring"**: BC Parks' page says this, but Bruce Peak (~709 m) is higher. Not used.
- **Garry oak meadow on Mount Galiano's summit**: only one blog said so.
- **Montague Harbour "first marine park in BC"**: not confirmed by BC Parks.
- **Salt Spring Apple Festival** (last Sunday of September per its site): real and current, but I picked the longer-running Fall Fair. Easy swap: events, months [9].
- **Pender Island farmers' market season**: sources conflict, so the item has no months.
- **Grouse Mountain grizzly bears / wind turbine**: current status not checked. Left out.
- **Tsawwassen First Nation treaty (2009)** and other Indigenous history, names and place names: left out under the brief. Places where official names now include Indigenous names (John Dean Park on Mount Newton; East Point's whale trail on Saturna) are flagged in the notes.
- **Business names**: only Butchart Gardens (a National Historic Site) is named. Sidney's aquarium and Grouse's Skyride are described without their names.
